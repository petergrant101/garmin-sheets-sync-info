Privacy policy · Effective 10 November 2026

Information stays with its purpose.

Garmin Sheets Sync is operated by its owner for personal use. It connects the owner’s Garmin information to the owner’s Google Sheets tracker.

Information the utility accesses

The utility reads available Garmin measurements, activity and wellness records. It reads and updates the configured Google spreadsheet, including saved nutrition entries, food references, summaries and sync status. Google authorisation is requested through Google’s consent screens; the utility does not ask for the owner’s Google password.

Use of Google user data

Google Sheets data is used to inspect existing records, preserve history, save updates, calculate tracker views and verify the saved results. The utility does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or to train general-purpose AI models. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and access

Tracker records are stored in the owner’s Google account and in local working files or caches used by the desktop utility. Google authorisation tokens and Garmin credentials are kept in private local computer settings, separate from this public website. Working tracker files may also be held in storage services the owner has configured, such as personal file synchronisation or backups.

The utility communicates with Google and Garmin to perform the requested synchronisation. It does not publish tracker records to this website. If the owner separately shares a spreadsheet or supplies records to an assistant or another service, that is a separate action controlled by the owner and governed by that service’s terms and privacy practices.

Retention, deletion and revocation

Records remain in the tracker and local storage until the owner removes them. Revoking Google access stops future authorised API access but does not delete existing spreadsheet records, caches or backups. The owner can revoke access through Google Account connections, stop the Windows sync task, and delete local application files or tracker records as needed. Backups and synchronised copies must be handled separately.

This public website

These static pages contain no forms, analytics scripts, advertising or app sign-in functionality. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process technical visit information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Public repository content and its history are visible on GitHub.

Contact and changes

The operator is the owner of the petergrant101 GitHub account. Use the contact options available on that profile for non-sensitive enquiries. Do not submit private health information or credentials publicly. Material changes to this policy will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
