Garmin Sheets Sync

A private personal-use sync utility that keeps its operator’s Garmin activity and wellness data alongside a personal nutrition tracker in Google Sheets.

What the app does

  • Imports available Garmin measurements, daily wellness readings and recorded activities into the operator’s existing spreadsheet.
  • Maintains saved food references, nutrition summaries and plant-diversity views within that spreadsheet.
  • Runs privately in Google Cloud at 08:00, 16:00 and 22:00 Europe/London, and reports when an update succeeds or needs attention. A paused Windows setup is retained for recovery.

Why Google access is requested

The cloud worker uses a dedicated Google service identity that the owner has granted Editor access to the existing tracker. It reads records and saves verified updates to that configured workbook. Browser-approved desktop access may be retained for recovery; those permissions can cover other spreadsheets, but the configured destination remains the personal tracker.

About this website

This is a public information site, not a health dashboard or sign-in service. It does not display the operator’s health records, spreadsheet address, credentials or private application configuration.
