A utility for personal record keeping.

Garmin Sheets Sync is a personal desktop utility operated by its owner. This website provides information about that utility; it does not offer public accounts or a hosted health-data service.

Purpose and access

The utility imports available Garmin records into the owner’s configured Google Sheets tracker and maintains saved nutrition records and derived views. The operator must have permission to access the connected accounts and records and remains responsible for account security and spreadsheet sharing settings.

Data limitations

Updates depend on the computer being awake, network availability, valid authorisation and the data made available by Garmin and Google. Records may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. Device readings and calculated values are estimates where labelled; a successful sync does not establish that a day’s food diary is complete.

Personal health use

The utility supports personal record keeping. It is not a medical device or a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Decisions should account for missing data and the limits of wearable and nutrition estimates.

Availability and control

No continuous availability or error-free operation is promised. The owner can stop the scheduled sync, revoke connected access and remove records. Stopping or revoking access does not automatically delete existing records or backups.

Privacy and third-party services

Data handling is described in the privacy policy. Google, Garmin and GitHub provide their own services under their own terms. This independently operated utility is not affiliated with or endorsed by those organisations.

Contact

For non-sensitive enquiries, use the contact options available on the operator’s GitHub profile. Do not post private health records or credentials publicly.
